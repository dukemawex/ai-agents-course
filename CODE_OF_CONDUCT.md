# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this course to be a welcoming place to learn, whatever your background, experience, age, gender identity, disability, ethnicity, nationality, religion, sexual orientation, or level of English. Beginners are especially welcome. Every question is a good question.

## What we expect

- Be kind, patient and respectful, in issues, pull requests, reviews and any course community space.
- Assume good intent, and explain rather than criticise.
- Give credit, and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Respect privacy: do not share other people's personal information, code or keys.
- Use what you learn here responsibly. Red-team and security exercises are for systems you own or have permission to test.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Threats, or encouraging anyone to harm themselves or others.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Using course material to attack systems you do not have permission to test.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainers privately through the GitHub profile of [@dukemawex](https://github.com/dukemawex). Please do not post details in a public issue. Reports will be handled promptly and kept confidential as far as possible.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful. The response will depend on how serious and how repeated the behaviour is.

## Scope

This code applies in all course repositories and in any space where someone represents the course.
